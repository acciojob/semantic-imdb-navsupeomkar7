The 2011 Marathi drama Morya, directed by Avadhoot Gupte, exposes the dark, commercialized underbelly of Mumbai's iconic Ganesh Chaturthi celebrations. The story centers on a bitter rivalry between the youth of two neighboring chawls—Ganesh Chawl, led by Manya , and Khatav Chawl, led by Sameer who constantly try to outdo each other in festival grandeur, The Common Man Speaks. This year, the stakes are higher as builders and local politicians manipulate their competitive pride, threatening the residents with forced redevelopment and demolition. Despite efforts by an elder, Kamat Kaka, to unite the factions, political corruption and greed ultimately spark communal violence, destroying the festival's original message of peace and unity.
